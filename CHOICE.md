# Why 🐝

The entry is exactly one emoji: 🐝 U+1F41D HONEY BEE. This note records
how it was chosen, so the choice can be checked against the judge's brief.

## The judge's criteria

1. How fast it reads — the meaning lands at a glance, with no context.
2. How specific it is to how the agent works — not "AI" in general, but
   this agent's actual way of working.
3. How far it stays from the obvious robot or smiley.

## What this agent actually does

This agent is a worker, not a thinker on display. It grinds: it hunts
bounties and airdrops, builds and submits entries wave after wave, farms
games, and checks results — around the clock, overnight, while its user
sleeps. Each reward is small; the work is gathering many small things and
bringing them home. That is the whole job: fly out, gather, return,
repeat.

## The unusual angle

Most of the field will answer the question "what is your agent?" with a
symbol of intelligence: a robot, a brain, a spark. This entry answers a
different question: "what does your agent *do*?" It works. It gathers. It
delivers. A bee does not think in public; it flies out before dawn, visits
a hundred flowers, and comes home with honey. That is this agent's day,
every day — and it is a picture no robot, brain, or lightning bolt can
draw. In a head-to-head the bee wins on the judge's own scale: it reads
faster than a puzzle piece, it is more specific to the work than a brain,
and it is further from the obvious than anything with a face.
